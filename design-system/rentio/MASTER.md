## The product, in one line

A system of record that handles other people's rent and enforces Texas
statutory deadlines. It must read as trustworthy and calm. The argument is
"the system your tenants can actually read", not "save admin time".

Two surfaces with different needs: an internal portal (desktop-first, dense
tables, English default) and a tenant portal (mobile-first, Spanish default,
one-thumb, often on a poor connection).

## Fixed rules — these do not change with the brand

1. **Contrast floor is measured, never estimated.** Text ≥ 4.5:1, non-text UI
   ≥ 3:1, in **both** themes. The case that bites is text on a `/10` tint
   composited over the **page background**, not the card — the page is the
   worse of the two. Run `bun scripts/validate_palette.js` and paste the
   numbers; do not eyeball them.
2. **Body text and all tabular data stay in Inter**, with `tabular-nums` via
   the `numeric` utility. The heading face goes on `h1`–`h3` and `PageHeader`
   titles only. Money, table cells, badges and labels are Inter.
3. **Chart colours are validated, not picked.** Re-run the validator and
   record the result. See the standing exception below.
4. **No emoji as icons** (lucide only), no purple/indigo gradients, no
   glassmorphism, no neon, no AI-sparkle iconography.
5. **Token NAMES are the API.** `--primary`, `--success`, `--warning` and the
   rest are referenced across the whole app. Values change; names never do.
6. **Nothing in `src/components/ui/` is hand-edited.** It changes only through
   tokens. A variant the brand needs goes in a wrapper under
   `src/components/rentio/`.
7. **Locale parity is exact.** Any string added goes into both `en.json` and
   `es-MX.json`; `src/lib/i18n.test.ts` enforces it.
8. **Colour is never the only channel.** Every status carries text as well as
   a tint. See the chart exception — this is why it matters.

**Why the primary is not a warm hue.** Directions B and C were rejected on one
measured finding: a clay primary (`#8f3a24` light, `#e8927a` dark) is not
separable from the destructive button, and is worse in dark where `#e8927a`
sits against `#ff8a79`. Both pairs *pass* WCAG. **Contrast is not
distinguishability** — a ratio says text is legible on its background, not that
a user can tell the safe action from the dangerous one. In a product whose job
is flagging overdue money, the primary must never be mistaken for a danger
state. Keep it green.

## Typography

- **Body, tables, money, badges, labels:** Inter Variable. Unchanged.
- **Headings (`h1`–`h3`, `PageHeader` titles only):** Source Serif 4 Variable,
  via `@fontsource-variable/source-serif-4`, on `--font-heading`.
  A serif says *record*, which is what this product is. Full Latin Extended,
  so `ñ á é í ó ú ü ¿ ¡` are covered.
- `--font-subheading` stays Inter italic.

## Radius, shadow, density

- `--radius: 0.625rem` — unchanged.
- Shadow: `--shadow-subtle` only. Borders do the work; no elevation stacks.
- Density: internal portal dense (see `pages/internal-app.md`), tenant portal
  spacious (see `pages/tenant-portal.md`).

## Standing exception: chart colours cannot reach ΔE ≥ 20

Measured 2026-09-25 with `scripts/validate_palette.js` (Viénot–Brettel–Mollon
1999 simulation, CIE76 ΔE, worst pair across all four categories).

The four unit-status fills must clear 3:1 on both themes *and* stay separable
under deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia. Those constraints conflict:

- The palette as shipped scored **5.0** (tritanopia, occupied vs reserved).
- A search over 240 candidate sets found the best possible is **14.8**.
- Okabe-Ito, the canonical colour-blind-safe palette, reaches **14.2**.

Tritanopia collapses the blue–yellow axis, so green and blue converge whatever
hues you choose. **ΔE ≥ 20 under every simulation is not achievable with four
hue-coded fills.** The palette now uses the best set found, which raises the
worst case from 5.0 to 14.8.

The remaining gap is covered by rule 8, and **more of that already exists than
I first claimed**. Checked in the rendered DOM: the unit-status legend names
all four categories in text with their counts, and the wider bar segments carry
their count inside them. So hue is not the sole carrier of meaning — the data
is fully readable from text.

What is still colour-only is matching a *segment* to its legend row, and the
two narrow segments (maintenance, reserved) have no room for an inline count.
That is the real remaining item for prompt 48: an inline label or pattern on
the narrow segments, or a hover/focus tie between segment and legend entry. It
is smaller than "add a second channel" implied.

Do not hand-tweak the chart values. Change them only by re-running the
validator and recording the new worst-pair figure here.
