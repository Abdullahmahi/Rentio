# Internal portal — overrides MASTER.md

Staff, desktop-first, English default. The job is getting through a month's
invoicing without missing a statutory deadline.

## Density
- Table rows 40px. Spacing scale 8/12/16/24/32 — the dense end.
- `DataTable` page size 15–20. Zebra striping on.
- Sidebar: three groups, collapsible to a rail.

## Emphasis
The dashboard exists for the things that cost money if missed. These must
break out of the calm everyday surface:
- the amber "N payments await confirmation" strip
- "Deposits due" (§92.103, 30-day clock)
- "H&S repairs past 7 days" (§92.056)
- "Turnover tasks overdue" (§92.156)

Everything else on the dashboard is reference, and should recede.

## Typography
- `PageHeader` title: `--font-heading`. Nothing else on the page.
- Money columns: Inter, `numeric`, right-aligned. Never the heading face.

## Breakpoints to check
1280px and 768px, light and dark.
