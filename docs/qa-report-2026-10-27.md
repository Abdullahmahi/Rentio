Rentio QA Report
Functional test of rentio-app.com (admin app)  ·  October 27, 2026  ·  Tested as Alejandro Hayen (Administrator)
Summary
I tested every page in the admin app using a fake property called &quot;Testing&quot;, with its own unit, tenants, leases, payment, expense and work order. I did not change any real tenant or lease data. Most screens and forms work. The problems that matter most are in billing and lease lifecycle: October rent was never invoiced, expired leases still show as Active, renewals create overlapping leases, and one tenant profile shows the wrong lease and balance. Authentication is also running on Clerk development keys.
Critical: 7 critical / high-priority issues
Medium: 12 functional bugs or missing functions
Low: 12 UX and polish items
1. Critical / high priority
#
Area / function
What happens
How to reproduce / where
1
Login / authentication (Clerk)
The browser console warns on every page that Clerk is loaded with DEVELOPMENT keys. Development instances have strict usage limits and are not meant for production, so logins or sign-ups can start failing as usage grows.
Open any page and check the browser console. Fix: switch to Clerk production keys and a production instance.
2
Contracts: lease status
Leases past their end date stay &quot;Active&quot;. Truman 101, 102, 103 and 104 ended 10/10/2026 but still show Active, do not appear under the &quot;Ended&quot; filter, and are not counted in &quot;Leases expiring&quot;.
Contracts list. Filter by Ended: only Ysleta 203 and the test lease appear.
3
Receipts / rent invoicing
No October 2026 rent receipts exist. Receipts are only created when someone clicks &quot;Generate this month&apos;s receipts&quot;, so this month nobody was billed. Knock-on effects: Dashboard &quot;Collected this month&quot; shows $0 of $0, delinquency ages are frozen at 87 days, and a new lease starting 10/01 had no receipt (the payment was held as credit).
Payments &gt; Receipts &gt; October 2026 is empty. Recommend auto-generating receipts on each lease&apos;s payment day. (I did not click Generate because it would bill every real lease.)
4
Contracts: Renew
Renewing a lease defaults the new start date to today rather than the day after the current lease ends, and the old lease stays Active. Result: two overlapping Active leases on the same unit. After a move-out on the renewal, the unit shows &quot;Vacant&quot; but still lists a current tenant.
Testing lease (10/01/2026 to 09/30/2027) &gt; Renew &gt; accept defaults. Contracts now lists two Active T1 leases.
5
Tenants: profile shows wrong lease and balance
Eduardo Barrera Lima&apos;s tenant page and the Tenants list show Truman 103, $830 rent, $0 balance. His actual lease is Truman 104, $1,170, with a $2,497 overdue balance (shown on Dashboard and Contracts). The tenant record is linked to the wrong lease, possibly because he is a co-tenant on 103.
Tenants &gt; Eduardo Barrera Lima, then compare with Contracts &gt; Truman 104.
6
Language switch
The ES/EN toggle in the top bar does nothing. In Settings &gt; Preferences, changing Spanish to English saves (the server returns success) but the screen stays in Spanish until a full page reload.
Click ES in the header: nothing changes. Settings &gt; Preferences &gt; English: still Spanish until refresh.
7
Dashboard: deposit alert
The red &quot;1 deposit past its statutory deadline&quot; banner links to the Reports page (income vs. expenses), not to the deposit or lease that needs action.
Dashboard &gt; click the red banner.

Maintenance cost to expenses

Delete property
A property with leases and payments can be deleted with only a generic confirmation. There is no warning about active leases or financial history. The message also says &quot;1 units&quot;.

9
Missing edit/delete functions
Units: no Edit, Delete or status change (Maintenance, Reserved). Tenants: no Edit or Delete. Work orders: no Delete, and priority, category and H&amp;S cannot be changed after creation. Leases: only the rent is editable, and the rent pencil still appears on ended leases.
Unit, tenant, work order and lease detail pages.
10
Late fees
Overdue receipts do not get late fees automatically. Each one needs a manual &quot;Apply late fee&quot; click.
Receipts &gt; REC-00061 (overdue since 09/05).
11
Documents only in Spanish
Rent receipt PDF (&quot;Recibo de renta&quot;), notice to vacate (&quot;Aviso para desocupar&quot;) and deposit disposition PDFs are Spanish-only, even when the app is in English. The CSV export file is named &quot;pagos-...csv&quot;.
Download any of these PDFs, or Payments &gt; Export CSV.
12
Move-out
After a move-out, the lease end date stays at the original end (10/26/2027) instead of the surrender date. The move-out form does not ask for a forwarding address (it is a separate step afterwards).
Record move-out on any lease.
